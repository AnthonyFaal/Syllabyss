Sidebar UI Tabs Derived from the URD
Here’s how I’d define the sidebar tabs:

1. Dashboard
Personalized overview (progress, recommendations, goals)

Quick links to continue learning

Notifications or insights

2. Courses / Learning Path
Browse All Courses

My Learning Path (AI-suggested tracks)

Microlearning Modules (on-demand)

3. Challenges & Rewards
Gamified challenges

Leaderboards

Badges, XP, and unlocked content

4. AI Assistant
Chatbot access (Help, Support, Guidance)

“Ask Me Anything” learning queries

Troubleshooting and learning recommendations

5. Collaboration Hub
Group Discussions / Forums

Study Groups (AI-matched peers)

Project-based or case study collaborations

6. Insights & Reports
My Learning Behavior

Efficiency Tips (AI-generated)

Educator Feedback (if applicable)

7. Content Studio (Visible only to Educators/Admins)
AI Course Generator

Quiz Builder

Simulation & Case Study Generator

8. Accessibility Settings
Text-to-speech / Speech-to-text

Font / Contrast adjustments

Audio learning toggle

9. Admin Panel (Admins only)
User Management

AI Performance Logs

Content Quality Review

10. Settings & Profile
Language & Localization

Personal Info

Notification Preferences
